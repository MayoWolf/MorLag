# Code of Conduct

MorLag is an open-source project, and we want participation here to feel respectful, safe, and constructive for everyone.

## Our Commitment

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. We are committed to making participation in this project harassment-free and focused on collaboration, learning, and shared problem-solving.

## Expected Behavior

When participating in this project, please:

- Be respectful and considerate in language and tone.
- Assume good intent, and respond to disagreement with curiosity.
- Offer constructive feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Help create an environment where people feel comfortable asking questions and contributing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable in this project:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Doxxing, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Sharing private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and any other public or private interactions connected to the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through the repository's communication channels. Reports should be handled promptly and respectfully, with care for the safety and privacy of the people involved.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Consequences

Possible responses to violations may include:

- A private warning or request to stop the behavior.
- Removal of content or contributions.
- Temporary suspension from project spaces.
- Permanent ban from future participation.
